# Start Here: Pathways Professional Development Radar

This page is for coworkers who want to use the radar without learning GitHub or programming.

## What is this?

The Pathways Professional Development Radar is a searchable list of professional-development, education, pathway, research, and health-care opportunities.

It is designed to answer a simple question:

> What opportunities are available to our team, and which ones are worth taking a closer look at?

The database can include conferences, workshops, courses, continuing education, calls for abstracts, advising resources, pathway programs, bridge programs, K–12 health-care career resources, medical education opportunities, and related professional development.

## The easiest way to use it

**1. Open the dashboard.**

Use the dashboard rather than browsing the repository files. The dashboard is the user-facing part of the project.

**2. Search for a topic.**

Try terms such as:
- medical education
- advising
- nursing
- counseling
- AI
- simulation
- research
- health equity
- K–12
- pathway
- professionalism
- competency-based education

**3. Use filters.**

You can narrow results by Category, Format, Cost, Region, Continuing education, and CFP / abstract opportunities.

**4. Open the official source.**

Every opportunity should link to the official source. Use that source to confirm current registration details before acting.

## What the labels mean

**Pathways relevant** means the opportunity has been identified as potentially relevant to the Pathways team's work. This is a relevance interpretation, not a claim made by the event organizer.

**CE available** means the source indicates some form of continuing education or professional credit is available.

**CFP / abstract** means the opportunity includes a call for abstracts, presentations, posters, submissions, or a similar contribution opportunity.

**Price not listed** means the source did not provide a verified price when the opportunity was checked. Do not assume that means free.

**Deadline not listed** means a registration or submission deadline was not found on the verified source.

## What the dashboard does not mean

The radar is a discovery tool, not an automatic recommendation system.

A listing means that the opportunity was found and its available details were checked against a source. It does not mean Pathways should attend, apply, submit, or spend money.

## How freshness works

The active dashboard is intentionally forward-looking.

Completed events and fully closed opportunities should be removed from the active database. An opportunity with multiple dates can remain active when a meaningful future date, application deadline, registration window, or submission deadline is still open.

Each record also has a verification date so maintainers can see when its details were last checked.

## If you want to suggest an opportunity

Send the maintainer:
- Opportunity name
- Organization
- Official webpage
- Date or deadline, if listed
- Any known cost or CE information

The official webpage is especially important because it lets the maintainer verify the information before adding it.
