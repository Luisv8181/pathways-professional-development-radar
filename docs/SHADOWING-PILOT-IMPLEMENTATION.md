# Shadowing Network — 90-Day Pilot Plan

**Pilot target:** 5 Pathways students, 3 confirmed host partners, and a coordinator-owned readiness process. The numbers are planning targets, not commitments from any named organization.

## Pilot objective

Demonstrate that Pathways can move a student from career interest to a completed, supervised exposure experience while meeting host, school, guardian, privacy, and safety requirements.

## Days 1–15: Governance and setup

- Name the executive sponsor and operational coordinator.
- Obtain approval from relevant school/district leadership and institutional privacy, legal, compliance, and risk-management contacts.
- Identify the approved system for student intake, parent/guardian permission, secure document exchange, clearance status, and retention.
- Decide which age/grade bands the pilot serves.
- Define permitted experiences: career conversations and non-clinical tours first; clinical shadowing only when the host expressly approves the age group and safeguards.
- Confirm student transportation and accessibility support.
- Adopt the requirements matrix and readiness gate.

**Gate 1:** No student intake begins until the school/program has approved the process and secure channels.

## Days 16–30: Recruit partners

- Identify 8–12 prospective departments/organizations across different career areas.
- Send the partner invitation and schedule scoping calls.
- Ask each host for written age eligibility, experience types, requirements, supervision, capacity, privacy and safety rules, accessibility process, and authorized contact.
- Obtain permission before publicly listing an opportunity.
- Select 3 partners only after written confirmation.

**Gate 2:** No opportunity is labeled “accepting interest” until the host has confirmed current capacity and the intake process.

## Days 31–45: Student orientation and matching

- Invite eligible students and explain that interest is not a placement.
- Collect requests only through the approved secure school channel.
- Review fit, age, schedule, transportation, accessibility, and student goals.
- Match students to a host-approved experience.
- Issue a site-specific requirements checklist and explain who completes each item.
- Obtain guardian/school approvals where required.

**Gate 3:** Do not schedule until required paperwork has been received and verified through approved channels and the host confirms clearance.

## Days 46–75: Experiences

- Confirm dates, supervisor, arrival instructions, attire, ID, transportation, emergency contact, and cancellation plan.
- Conduct student orientation on observer-only boundaries, confidentiality, patient choice, no recording, and escalation.
- Keep the student with the assigned supervisor.
- Document completion status only in the approved system.
- Pause immediately if host rules, safety, consent, or privacy are unclear.

## Days 76–90: Evaluation

Collect aggregate metrics:
- Partners contacted, verified, and accepting interest
- Student requests, matches, cleared students, and completed experiences
- Median days from request to confirmed schedule
- Cancellations and reasons
- Student career clarity and experience feedback
- Host satisfaction and willingness to host again
- Barriers such as transportation, cost, age limits, scheduling, and accessibility

Do not publish identifiable student reflections or individual clearance status.

## Pilot success criteria

- Three host partners provide written approval and clear requirements.
- Five students complete an approved career-exposure experience, or documented barriers explain why the target was not met.
- 100% of placements pass the readiness gate before attendance.
- No student-level sensitive documents are stored in GitHub or the public Radar.
- Students and hosts report that coordination and expectations were clear.

## Stop / pause criteria

Pause a placement if the host's age rules, supervision, paperwork, patient-consent process, privacy expectations, insurance/affiliation requirements, or emergency process are unclear. Escalate to the program sponsor and relevant institutional authority.
