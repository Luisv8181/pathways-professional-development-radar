# Pathways Shadowing Network — Partner Outreach Kit

## Initial email

**Subject:** Invitation to partner with Pathways on student career-shadowing experiences

Hello [Name],

I coordinate Pathways, a program helping students explore education and career pathways in healthcare and related fields. We are developing a small, carefully supervised shadowing and career-exposure network so students can learn directly from professionals and understand the range of roles that support patient care and community health.

Would your department or organization be open to discussing a pilot? We are interested in the format that works best for your team: a career conversation, workplace tour, simulation/lab observation, or—where your policies permit—a structured job-shadow experience.

Pathways would coordinate student preparation, communication, scheduling, and required school/guardian steps. We would follow your organization's age limits, visitor policies, confidentiality requirements, patient-consent procedures, clearance requirements, and supervision rules. Students would be observers only and would not provide care or access records.

Would you be available for a brief 15-minute conversation to explore whether there is a safe and practical fit? We would appreciate learning about eligible ages, available roles, timing, capacity, and the current process for approval and paperwork.

Thank you for considering an opportunity to help students see what a future in healthcare can look like.

Best,
[Coordinator name]
Pathways
[Program contact information]

## Scoping-call questions

1. Does your organization accept high-school students for career exposure or shadowing? What minimum age/grade applies?
2. Which formats are permitted: career conversation, tour, simulation, non-clinical observation, clinical observation?
3. Which departments/professions are appropriate for minors?
4. What are the required approvals, forms, clearances, health documentation, training, and deadlines?
5. Who is the authorized contact for each requirement, and where must documents be submitted?
6. What are the privacy, patient-consent, infection-prevention, dress, ID, device, and restricted-area rules?
7. What is the supervision ratio, visit length, group size, and number of students per term?
8. Are there costs, transportation/parking issues, or accessibility arrangements?
9. What is the cancellation and escalation process?
10. Can the partner provide written permission to list the opportunity and a date for re-verification?

## Partner opportunity intake fields

- Partner organization and department
- Authorized contact role and official contact details
- Official source / policy URL
- Opportunity title and experience type
- Professions / career areas
- Age and grade eligibility
- Dates, frequency, duration, capacity, and current availability
- Site location and arrival/parking instructions
- Requirement checklist and responsible party for each item
- Patient-consent and observer scope rules
- Supervision and emergency/escalation contacts
- Accessibility and accommodation contact/process
- Transportation / cost notes
- Approval to list publicly (yes/no; approver; date)
- Last verified date and next verification date

## Follow-up after a pilot

Thank the partner, share a short aggregate summary (number of students, experience type, and feedback themes), ask what should change, and confirm whether the partner is willing to host another cohort. Do not share identifiable student reflections without permission.
